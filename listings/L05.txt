Floodplain and Wetlands, Read Before Purchasing: This parcel is located within a FEMA Zone AE floodplain. Residential and other structures are permitted, but the lowest floor must be elevated at least 2 feet above the FEMA Base Flood Elevation (BFE), and all structures must be properly anchored against flotation, collapse, and lateral movement. Basements are not permitted. Enclosed areas below the required elevation are generally limited to parking, access, or storage and must include compliant flood openings. The exact BFE, floodway boundary, buildable envelope, required elevations, and wetland limits must be confirmed through a survey and engineering review before any construction, septic placement, or site planning.

A well must be drilled and a septic system must be installed, neither is currently present on the property. Placement of septic systems may be affected by floodplain and wetland conditions and will require county review.
