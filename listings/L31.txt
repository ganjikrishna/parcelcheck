16218 NW MASON Rd, North Plains, OR 97133. Size: 21.42 Acres.

Build your dream home on this absolutely stunning 21.42-acre horse property on sought-after Mason Hill Rd! A finalized building permit for a future residence is in place with Washington County, and a new septic system and perc test have been approved. A new well was installed in 2023. The property also has electricity already brought into multiple locations. Originally developed as a premier equestrian facility in 2019, this unique property is fully fenced and cross-fenced, with 200 feet of serene Jackson Creek frontage. The impressive 19-stall barn is 42' x 140' and features oversized 12x14 fully matted stalls - 10 with attached runs - plus a wash rack, paddocks, and lush pastures for turnouts. A Tuff Shed serves as the tack room, and two utility 20' x 20' garages/workshops with power and abundant space for equipment or hay storage.
