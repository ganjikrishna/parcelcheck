TITLE: ParcelCheck: Land Diligence Checker That Finds Hidden Problems

ParcelCheck is a land diligence checker, solo entry by Vinay K Ganji Krishna in the Meta Muse Ember Hackathon, Scale the Real World, directed by him with AI assistance. Muse Ember wrote the code from specs and runs every check.

Two-minute demo: Florida listing hides no legal road access, Georgia tax sale runs live, and Miami Beach address-only check flags FEMA Zone AE from live public records: FEMA, USDA soil survey, US Fish and Wildlife wetlands, US Census geocoder.

Nine checks: legal road access, flood zone, water and well, liens and taxes, perc and septic, zoning, utilities, mineral and timber rights, survey status. Flags quote the source word for word. Missing text becomes a question to ask.

Verified against ten listings with a hash-sealed answer key: nine of eleven stated problems caught, no clean point flagged. Spend under thirty cents of ten dollars.
