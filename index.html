ParcelCheck

Know what the listing is not telling you, for pennies.

Paste a land listing, or type just an address, and get a nine point diligence report; every red flag quotes its source word for word; what the text never says becomes the questions to ask.

Runs on Muse Ember and each live check costs a fraction of a cent.

The nine points

  1. Legal road access
  2. Flood zone
  3. Perc test and septic
  4. Water source and well
  5. Liens and tax status
  6. Zoning
  7. Utilities at the lot
  8. Mineral and timber rights
  9. Survey and boundaries

Run the check

Your nine point diligence report will appear here. Paste a listing above, or choose a cached example.

How it works

  • One structured Muse Ember pass against a nine point checklist written from real land diligence.
  • A harness validates every answer before it is shown (nine points, quotes must appear word for word in the source text, flags and questions consistent).
  • When the text contains a US address, live public records (US Census geocoder, FEMA flood layer, USDA soil survey, US Fish and Wildlife wetlands inventory) are checked too and merged into the same report.
  • Records can fill silence but are never invented.
  • Honest limits: it reads text and records, it does not visit the land.
