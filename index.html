Land diligence, automated

ParcelCheck

Know what the listing is not telling you, for pennies.

Land listings are written to sell. The expensive facts, no legal road access, a flood zone, no perc test, are usually present only as silence. ParcelCheck reads a listing against nine diligence points, quotes its source for every flag, names everything left unsaid, and writes the exact questions to ask the seller. And because listings go stale, whenever an address is in the text it also checks live public records: FEMA flood maps, USDA soils, federal wetlands. Type just an address and the records are the check. One check costs about a fifth of a cent on Muse Ember.
